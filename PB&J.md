# Teach a robot to make a PB & J sandwich

## Assumptions
- We have a fully charged humanoid robot
- We have a countertop with the  following items:
    - Butter Knife
    - Jar of PB
    - Jar of Jelly
    - 2 slices of bread laid side by side on a plate


## Steps to Follow
1. Position self in front of itmes
2. Open the jar of PB
3. Open the jar of jelly
4. Grab the knife
5. Use the kinfe to scoop out some PB
6. Pick up the bread and spread PB over flat sutface of the bread
7. Put PB bread back onto plate
8. Use the kinfe to scoop out some jelly
9. Pick up the bread and spread jelly over flat sutface of the bread
10. Put down the knife
11. Place jelly bread onto PB bread with the covered surfaces together
12. Replace the lids on the jars
13. Put jars and knife into the right spots


## Done When
- Sandwich is constructed
- Area must be clear except for plate and sandwich
